Three steps to your weekly schedule

  1. Fill in the schedule form

    One per line with its day and time, such as Math 101 - Mon 9:00 AM. Games can name the opponent.

  2. Set up to 50 study hours a week

    Then pick your school level, your training intensity and the areas you want tips on.

  3. Read the schedule and the plans

    Four tabs: the weekly overview, workout plans, study plans and tips. Export saves it all as one file.

Does StuAth.ai cost anything?

No. It is free and open source under the MIT licence.

Where does StuAth.ai keep my answers?

In this browser’s storage. There is no account to make.

How does the planner place each session?

By fixed rules: workouts on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, rest on Sunday morning.

Which sports get drills in the planner?

Basketball and football. Other sports get a general plan for now.

Which languages does StuAth.ai come in?

English, Spanish, French and Chinese. Study plans and tips stay in English.
